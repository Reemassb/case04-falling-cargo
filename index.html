CASE EVIDENCE RECORDED
CASE #04

The Falling Cargo

At 02:14 a loaded container came off the spreader of a yard gantry crane and fell to the deck. The yard was clear. Nobody was hurt, and the container was destroyed.

The crane operator's union says the clamp failed and the equipment was at fault. The terminal operator says the trolley was being run above the posted speed and the load was swinging.

The trolley carries a position encoder that logs where it was at the instant the clamp released. The impact centre has been surveyed along the same rail axis. Those two numbers are the case.

Nobody disputes the container fell. The question is how fast it was travelling sideways when it let go — because a container released from a stationary trolley lands directly beneath the release point.
